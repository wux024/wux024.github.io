I am currently with the [School of Ocean Information Engineering](https://oceaninfo.jmu.edu.cn/), [Jimei University](https://www.jmu.edu.cn/), located in the beautiful coastal city of Xiamen. I obtained my bachelor's degree in 2019 and master's degree in 2022 from the same university, under the supervision of Prof. [Lianming Wang](https://hxy.hntou.edu.cn/szdw/zrjs/gjzc/202310/t20231023_80854.html). Prior to joining Jimei University, I worked at the Intelligent Perception and Optoelectronic Information Processing Group in Changchun.
