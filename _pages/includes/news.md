# News
{: lang="en"}

- **Oct 2026** — [AnimalPoseTracker](https://github.com/wux024/AnimalPoseTracker) is released as open-source software (GPL-3.0): cross-species animal pose estimation with two model families, the full workflow from labelling to deployment, and multi-camera 3D. The [documentation](https://wux024.github.io/AnimalPoseTracker/) is online.
{: lang="en"}
