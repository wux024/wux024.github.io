    Optoelectronic Information Processing & Intelligent Perception

    • Ph.D. graduate from Northeast Normal University, working on optoelectronic information processing, intelligent perception, and animal pose estimation.
    • Changchun, China

    I previously worked at the Intelligent Perception and Optoelectronic Information Processing Group in Changchun. I graduated from School of Physics, Northeast Normal University with a bachelor’s degree in 2019 and a master’s degree in 2022, advised by Prof. Lianming Wang. I received my Ph.D. from School of Physics, Northeast Normal University in June 2026, advised by Prof. Huang Jipeng. My research interests are in optoelectronic information processing and intelligent perception, and I am currently working on animal pose estimation.

    Research Interests

    • Cross-species pose estimation and applications
    • Underwater visual perception
    • Edge intelligence
